## The problem

Most crypto wallets either overwhelm users with jargon or hide so much that power users don't trust them. The middle ground — clear, scannable, and safe — is surprisingly rare.

## What I built

A wallet UI designed around clarity: every transaction shows its effect in plain language before the user signs, and advanced details are one click away for those who want them.

## Stack & decisions

- **Frontend:** Next.js + TypeScript, dark-first design language.
- **UX:** confirmation screens written in human terms ("You'll send X, receive Y") instead of raw hex.
- **Safety:** visual warnings for unusual approvals or unknown contracts.

## Outcome

A wallet interface that feels more like Stripe than a blockchain explorer — proving crypto UX can compete with modern fintech.
