## The problem

Most e-commerce UIs default to a cluttered marketplace aesthetic — every product competing for attention, no coherent visual identity. Shoppers land and feel overwhelmed rather than inspired.

## What I built

ShopCo is a clean, brand-forward storefront built around the idea that finding clothes that match your style should feel effortless. The catalog spans 200+ international brands with high-quality products, built to surface the right item to the right shopper without noise.

## Stack & decisions

- **Frontend:** Next.js + Tailwind — fast iteration on layout with a consistent design language across every page.
- **Design:** typography-led, generous whitespace, letting the products breathe instead of fighting for space.
- **Performance:** static generation and optimized images for sub-second load times.

## Outcome

A storefront that feels more editorial than transactional — proving that e-commerce UI can have a point of view without sacrificing usability.
